Frequently asked questions

Does the product stay identical in the final visual?

Yes: Reskin keeps the product (shape, colours, label) and only transforms the setting, the person and the mood. Always check the result before publishing.

What do I need to get started?

The product image (from Alibaba or a photo of the product you received), optionally a reference photo of a person with African features, and one sentence describing the scene: skin tone, hairstyle, setting.

Can I choose the type of African features?

Yes. Describe the skin tone, hairstyle and setting that match your customers — a woman with ebony skin and braids, a mixed-race man in city wear, a market atmosphere… The tool adapts the staging while keeping your product unchanged.

Can I use the visual for my ads and my shop?

Yes. Generate in 9:16 for TikTok, Reels and WhatsApp statuses, and in 1:1 for your product page or your Facebook and Instagram posts.

What if I don't like the result?

Say the retouch in one sentence ("brighter background", "tighter crop") and regenerate from the tool below.
